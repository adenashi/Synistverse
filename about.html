Synist is a NSFW art project in the form of a deckbuilding incremental game about a fallen angel, his growing harem, and their attempts to resurrect the God of Pleasure.


Hello, there! My name is Adana. I'm an NSFW artist, writer, and solo game developer of Neon Noods Games. I love yaoi, card games and worldbuilding, so I'm combining them all in my dream project. Because I'm nearly forty, and it feels like I'm running out of time to actually do what I want.

The Synistverse is a collection of settings, characters, and lore that I've created while working on this project. It also holds information about the game that I'll be developing alongside all of the art and writing. This is a living document of sorts, and will be updated continuously as I flesh out the contents of the story and game.

I'll be doing most of the things in this endeavor:

  • Art
  • Writing
  • Worldbuilding
  • Game Design
  • Programming

If you'd like to follow my progress and support my work, I'd be super grateful if you become a member of my Patreon. That's where I'll be posting all of the NSFW things that will make up the majority of this project.

Or, if you'd like to stick to the Safe-For-Work bits, I'll be posting devlogs on YouTube. You can also follow me on Bluesky for random thoughts and updates.

In the meantime, feel free to explore Haenem and its inhabitants.

Until next time!
