Monxa trees are magical trees that feed on creative or sexual energy, and produce Monxa Fruit. When consumed, the fruit opens the internal pathways that allow for direct access to and manipulation of [aether](../Aether). Their core is an aether stone, which can be used as a fuel source by both [people](../Species) and [mechanics](../Aetherworks).
