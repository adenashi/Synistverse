## Syllable Structure and Stress

Structure: (C)-V-(C)

Stress Rule:
- By default, stress falls on second to last syllable
- Stress falls on last syllable when a homonym is a being used as a verb.

## Sentence Structure and Grammar

Sentence Structure : SVO
Subjects:
1. Subject preposition
2. Subject noun
3. Subject adjective

Verbs:
1. Auxiliary verb (helping verb) - a word that pairs with a main verb to show tense, mood, or voice
2. Lexical verb (main verb) - the core word that carries the actual meaning, action, or state in a sentence
3. Adverb - a word that modifies or describes a verb, an adjective, or another adverb

Objects:
1. Object preposition
2. Object noun
3. Object adjective

Possessor comes second, connected with possessive preposition (of).
