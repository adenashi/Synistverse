Angels are like a mix of Valkyrie and elves. The each have their own role, and were given a certain configuration of eyes in order to carry out their work. Some are psychopomps, while others are observers or scribes.

Every angel is spawned as an offspring of one of Inir's original seven, and share their progenitor's eye configuration and role.

While angels belong to Inir's Realm, they actually live in the physical realm. Inir's Realm is the name for the state of connection to Inir that allows angels to manipulate aether in the physical realm. As such, they are able to fly and perform magic when necessary. They tend to keep to themselves, preferring to focus on carrying out Inir's will.

When an angel fails to carry out Inir's will, or incurs the wrath of Inir or their progenitor, they are cast out of Inir's Realm. As a result, their angelic eyes are blinded, and they are cut off from Inir's aether pathways.
