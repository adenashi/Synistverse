Gods are beings formed from aether through the collective consciousness. There are multiple gods in the recognized pantheon of Haenem, though some have been forgotten -- or erased from history.

## Inir
Inir is the God of Endings, and takes responsibility for bringing things to completion. In order to carry out this mission, he created seven angels to help facilitate his work in the physical realm.

## Syna
 Syna is the Goddess of Pleasure, and is currently unable to intervene in the physical realm due to Inir's intervention during the Native War.
