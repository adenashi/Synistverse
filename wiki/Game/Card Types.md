There are five types of cards in Synist:

## Positions
Positions can only be played one at a time. When played, they reset the orgasm meters for all active participants in the ritual. They drain Thaen's stamina for as long as they are in play.

## Actions
Actions can be stacked on positions, or played on a companion.

## Stimulants
Can only be played when a position is not active on the board.

## Items
Can be played during pauses, or creates a pause when played.

## Participants
Can either be a companion of the harem, or a member of the audience. Audience members only show up in the deck when they come to the shrine for a ritual. Audience members can be leveled up through repeated use in order to convert them into permanent companions.
