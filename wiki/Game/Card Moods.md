There are five moods in Synist cards. Each is represented by a color, and has certain advantages and disadvantages.

## Relaxed (Teal)
Thaen will sit back and let his companions take the lead.
- Advantage: Low stamina drain rate
- Disadvantage: Lower energy generation

## Hungry (Orange)
Thaen will absorb his companions' energy in order to keep pushing them until they are all completely spent.
- Advantage: Replenish stamina after each orgasm
- Disadvantage: Less energy sent to tree

## Dominant (Black)
Thaen will be hard and rough, and will leave more than a few marks by the end of the ritual.
- Advantage: More energy generated by Thaen
- Disadvantage: Thaen orgasms faster

## Playful (Yellow)
Thaen will tease his companions until they can no longer keep it together.
- Advantage: More chances for energy multipliers
- Disadvantage: Less chance of audience participation

## Indulgent (Magenta)
Thaen will take his time, tasting every inch of his companions while putting on a show.
- Advantage: Higher chance of audience participation
- Disadvantage: Lower orgasm rate
