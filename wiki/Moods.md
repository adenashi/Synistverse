There are five moods that govern cards in Synist. Each is represented by a color, and has certain advantages and disadvantages.

## Relaxed
*Allows the Supplicant to take control of the pace.*

Advantage: Lower Stamina drain rate <br/>
Disadvantage: Lower Energy generation

## Playful
*Makes use of Items to ease the Supplicant into a more relaxed state.*

Advantage: More chances for Energy multipliers <br/>
Disadvantage: Fewer Positions

## Indulgent
*Involves lots of foreplay, building energy without full penetration.*

Advantage: Slower Stamina drain <br/>
Disadvantage: Lower Energy generation

## Hungry
*The Adherent feeds off the Supplicant's pleasure.*

Advantage: Replenishes Stamina<br/>
Disadvantage: Higher Focus cost

## Dominant
*Moves hard and rough, and can leave more than a few marks.*

Advantage: Lower Focus cost <br/>
Disadvantage: Fewer chances for Energy multipliers
