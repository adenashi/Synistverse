Angels are the children and messengers of the [nine gods](../The%20Nine%20Gods), and are marked by having more than two eyes. Some of them once acted as psychopomps, while others were priests and guards of the temples dedicated to their progenitors.

However, once the nine gods began to lose themselves and cause humanity to suffer, most of the angels turned their backs on them and offered their help to humans. As a result, the nine gods collectively stripped the angels of their wings and cursed them in hopes that humans would never accept them. As such, even angels born long after are still born with multiple eyes and scars were wings would have been.

During the God War, angels were initially distrusted, with many believing that they were only pretending to help when they were really going to betray humans at some point.  This led to many angels simply walking away, unwilling to force themselves into the situation and end up acting just as the gods did. 

It wasn't until Idrael, the eldest son of Inir, gave his life in order to save a human soldier during the Battle at Wolf Hill, that humans finally began to trust angels that stepped forward to help. His sacrifice not only disrupted Inir's plans and led to a human victory for the battle, but it the soldier that Idrael saved went on to save his king during another hard-won battle. 

This string of events came to be known as the Angel's Touch, something that would led the recipient to do something great. The idea lived on even after the God War, and many people sought out angels in order to receive their touch -- in whatever form it was given.
