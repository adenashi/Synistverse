A Baroque-inspired kingdom in a fantasy world where people have lost the ability to perform magic after killing the gods they unwittingly spawned, and are reliant on fading memory and limited knowledge of how The Collective works in order to maintain its competence-focused society.

## Calendar
There are only nine months in the year. Each month is 40 days long, making a year 360 days, and a season 90 days long. 

There is a leap year every six years. In Haenem, that extra day is called Sunkene, and is seen as a day for rest and honoring the dead and the knowledge they've left behind.

## Capital
The capital city of Haenem, where the [Synist Order](../The%20Synist%20Order) is currently located, is called Kyto. It means "alive" in the Haenish language, and tends to live up to that name. Its population grew due to the discovery of a [fluid](../Fluid) deposit nearby. The Wonxu family, a wealthy noble house that governed the region where the fluid deposit was found, quickly took control of it and founded the Wonxu Fluid Works. Nearly 80% of the capital's population now work for the Wonxu family in some capacity, despite the Genan family being the royal line.

## Culture
Many people of this world want to use [The Collective](../The%20Collective) to manifest their desires, but are also too afraid to do so and inadvertently cause something that others will blame them for. Therefore, most attempt to indirectly convince others to adapt their desires by creating and maintaining an image meant to win people over.
