Humans are the physical manifestations of pieces of the [Collective](../The%20Collective). As such, they are able to draw on the Collective in order to manifest their desires if they are aware of a method that works. However, when enough humans share the same desire, the Collective will fulfill it without prompting -- though no one may like the results.

Humans became aware of this when the [Nine Gods](../The%20Nine%20Gods) came into existence through such an occurrence. Initially, humans were awed by the gods' magical and otherworldly abilities -- and were enamored with being gifted the chance to use magic themselves. 

Those who experienced using magic were changed by it, causing their hair and eyes to take on extraordinary colors. This change remained in their bodies, and were passed down through their descendants. Even now, those with eyes and hair of strange colors are called 'magic-touched' and are unconsciously giving preferential treatment regardless of whether they have earned it.

But when the gods began to demand worship in increasingly disturbing ways, humans began to weigh the benefit of magic against the cost of keeping the gods appeased. When one king refused to offer Xril his daughter as a sacrifice, Xril slaughtered the entire family and lay waste to the capital. This event sparked the God War, which led to the eventual deaths of all nine gods and the destruction of the temples and worshippers that had gathered around them.

Since then, humans have become wary of triggering the Collective for any new gods. As such, they have become much more self-sufficient, and value competence and problem-solving more than anything. While the memory of magic still weighs on their collective minds, it is generally believed that there is a way for humans to gain magic again without giving birth to more gods.
