Demons are akin to energy vampires. They feed on the emotions and dreams of other species, collecting it as fuel that can be consumed in order to experience those emotions themselves at a later time.

They have horns of various shapes and sizes, and colorful skin and eyes, though they have black sclera.

They descend from the children of an ancient god who passed into the Collective long before the ones involved in the [God War](../The%20God%20War) were created. Humans were aware of the existence of demons the entire time. Views on them were usually split between seeing them as divine due to being connected to a deity, or as unnatural due to their lack of innate emotions. 

The latter view was only worsened as many demons found the more negative emotions far more appealing than positive ones. That preference led many to instigate and seek out negative emotions among human settlements.

During the God War, most demons sided with humans and actively helped fight against the gods and their worshippers. A good number of them made names for themselves as strategists and scouts, proving invaluable in gaining strategic advantages over the gods.

After the God War, demons were generally accepted as a proven ally to humanity. They were better able to live among humans, though a lot of them then had to deal with always being expected to be the smartest person in the room.
