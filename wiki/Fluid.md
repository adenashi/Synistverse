The byproduct of the buried, rotting corpses of the [Nine Gods](../The%20Nine%20Gods). When properly processes and harnessed, it allows the user to manifest effects that go beyond what's naturally possible.

A booming industry has formed around the control, processing, and distribution of fluid, leading many families to become outrageously wealthy and influential.
