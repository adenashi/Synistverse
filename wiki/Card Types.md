There are four card types in Synist, each with their own purpose and effect on the game.
### Positions
Position cards represent the current configurations of bodies, and how fast they're moving. Positions have a base number of seconds between Thrusts, a base amount of Energy generated as well as a base amount of Stamina drained every thrust.

Position cards can be played at any time during a round as long as the Adherent has remaining Stamina. Position cards are played in the Position slot, and replace any active card there. Playing a Position card clears all cards from the Body Grid, resets the Adherent's Focus, and locks the Body Grid cells indicated on the card. They deplete a certain number of seconds from the session time when played.

### Actions
Action cards represent things the Adherent can do to the Supplicant, or have the Supplicant do to them. They can generate Energy when played, or when on the Body Grid with an active Position card, and can affect Stamina and Focus.

To play them, the Adherent's indicated body part and one of the possible Body Grid cells indicated on the card must be free. They cost Focus to play, and deplete a certain number of seconds from the session time when placed on the Body Grid.

### Items
Item cards represent things the Adherent and Supplicant can use during a session. They can increase the Energy generated by surrounding cards when in play.

To play them, the indicated body part for the Adherent and the Supplicant must both be free. They cost Focus to play, deplete a certain number of seconds when placed on the Body Grid, and reduce the amount of Energy generated by any active Position card when played.

### Partners
Partner cards represent other Adherents who are joining the session. They can increase the Energy generated during the session.

They can be played even when the indicated Body Grid cell is locked, and will replace any card in the indicated cell. They cost Focus to play, but do not deplete seconds from the Session Timer.
