Gameplay in Synist revolves around "timed" sessions in the Libertorium. During these sessions, cards are played on the Body Grid in order to push the Supplicant to orgasm and generate enough Energy to fulfill the Supplicant's wish (and earn their Gold) before the session time runs out, while maintaining one's Stamina and Focus.

Adherents gain experience points during successful sessions, and can level up to increase Stamina, Focus, maximum daily sessions, and effects on sessions and cards.

Successfully bringing a Supplicant to orgasm generates Skill points, which can be used to upgrade individual cards.

The Order earns Gold for each successful session, which can be used to repair and level up the Temple to increase the length and payment for future sessions.

## Starting a Session
To start a session, the player must choose an Adherent who will perform during the session. Each Adherent has their own effect on certain cards that are played during a session, and a maximum number of sessions they can take part in during a single day.

A session starts with three possible rounds and the Adherent's full Stamina and Focus. A round ends when the Adherent runs out of Stamina.

## Session Time
Sessions have allotted amounts of seconds that are consumed when certain card types are played. The amount of time a session is given is proportionate to the amount of Energy that needs to be generated and the amount of Gold that can be earned if the Energy quota is met. Once the time runs out, the session ends, and the final results are tallied.

Sessions start with a three rounds (though some can have more). Rounds can last as long as necessary, as long as there is still time in the session. If all rounds are completed before the session time runs out, the session ends early, and the remaining time is deducted from the base Gold amount promised for the session.

If the Energy quota is not met, the session is considered a failure. The Adherent does not gain experience points and the Supplicant does not pay their Gold.

If the session's Energy quota is fulfilled while there are still rounds remaining, or if the session's Energy quota is surpassed, the player earns the chance to add a new card to their deck.

## The Body Grid
The Body Grid represents the body of the supplicant during the session. Each cell represents a body part, and have certain multipliers based on the erogenous zones of the supplicant.

As Adherents level up, they get better at quickly identifying the erogenous zones of supplicants, causing them to be revealed faster during a session. If an Adherent and Supplicant meet more than once, the Adherent will better remember the Supplicant's erogenous zones more and more with each session.

## The Orgasm Meter
The Supplicant's Orgasm Meter is affected by the amount and continuity of generated energy.

The meter increases by the number of cards on the Body Grid when the energy generated by the last played card is the equal to or higher than the amount generated by the one before. The meter decreases by five when the energy generated by the last played card is lower than the amount generated by the one before.

If the Orgasm Meter reaches zero and stays there for more than 300 seconds, the round ends.

When it reaches its max level, the Adherent earns a Skill Point and the meter is reset to zero, along with the last recorded energy amount.

## Focus and Stamina
Each Adherent has a certain amount of Focus and Stamina that they utilize during sessions.

Focus is used to play certain cards from the player's hand. Each hand begins with five cards. When the Adherent's Focus is depleted, a new Position card must be played in order to restore Focus. If there is no Position card in hand, a new hand can be drawn at the cost of one second per discarded card and that time's equivalent amount of Stamina from any active Position card.

Stamina is drained when a Position card is in play. It can be partially restored when other cards are played. When the Adherent's Stamina is depleted, the round ends.

## Playing Cards
Playing a card on the Body Grid serves two purposes:
1. Using the card itself
2. Simulating the passage of time.

Each card played depletes a number of seconds from the session, and triggers a reduction in Stamina if there is a Position card in play.

If there is Position card in play, all cards on played on the grid add to the energy generated by the Position card each time a new card is played. If there is no Position card in play, any card played will only generate its energy once before being discarded.

## Deck Management
The player starts with a deck of 12 cards. 

New cards can be added to the deck after excellent sessions (see above). Between sessions, the player can spend acquired Skill points to upgrade cards. Cards can be upgraded to:
- Increase Energy Output
- Increase Energy Multipliers
- Decrease Stamina Drain
- Decrease Focus Cost
- Add new effects
- Strengthen current effects on other cards

## Temple Leveling
The player can spend gold to purchase repairs for the temple. Each repair increases the temple's image by a certain amount.

Once the temple's image meets the next required threshold, the temple level increases. Increasing the temple's level allows for longer and/or higher-paying sessions.
